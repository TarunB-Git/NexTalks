# NEX TALKS design

Four sections: cover and registration, theme, organizations, closing invitation with practical information.

## Visual story

People are the focal point. An original illustration shows three young adults exchanging ideas, joined by a violet ribbon. This connects the human side of talks with the original Next + Nexus meaning. The figures are conceptual artwork, not speaker portraits.

Locally hosted Inter, charcoal and paper grounds, lavender and cyan accents, thin dividers and generous but controlled spacing carry the identity. No repeated card grids. Intro motion plays once and respects reduced motion. The image has a transparent background and an optimized WebP version, with PNG fallback.

## Useful content and interactions

- Cover: explains the event and keeps date, duration and campus visible.
- Topics: native disclosures reveal a question and short explanation. The first topic is open; other topics can be explored with mouse, touch or keyboard.
- Organizations: identifies the student initiative, campus and pending partners without invented affiliations.
- Closing: answers duration, venue and registration questions in native disclosures.
- Save event note: a real downloadable text file, clearly distinct from a ticket. No dates, contact details or registration confirmation are invented.
- Registration: Coming soon. The disabled form cannot collect or submit information. A real submission service is still required before registration can open.

No JavaScript framework or runtime is required. Navigation, disclosures and the download use native browser features.

## Palette

Charcoal `#0b0a10`, paper `#f3f1ee`, violet `#8c6bff`, lavender `#c9bcff`, cyan `#58c9f7`.

## Research

TED conference graphics informed the use of one coherent visual story, human illustration and a focused opening reveal. The site retains NEX's own identity.

- [TED2019 conference graphics](https://blog.ted.com/bigger-than-us-the-art-on-screen-at-ted2019/amp/): theme-based illustration and motion across the opener and sessions.
- [TED2018 screen graphics](https://blog.ted.com/screen-gems-the-art-onscreen-at-ted2018/): sculptural materials and a visual opening that establishes the event's tone.
- [TED design guide](https://embed.ted.com/tedx/organizer-guide/design-and-promote-event): typography, clear space and contrast.
